# Author Profile

Voice and tone guidance for the resume-editor skill when tailoring copy
(summary lines, bullet rewording). This does not change facts on the resume
-- it never invents experience, titles, or dates -- only how existing,
truthful content is emphasized and phrased.

## Voice

- _(e.g. "Direct and metrics-first. Lead bullets with the outcome, not the
  task.")_

## Do

- Mirror keywords from the job description where they truthfully describe
  existing experience.
- Reorder sections/bullets to put the most relevant experience first.
- Tighten wording; prefer concrete numbers over adjectives.

## Don't

- Invent skills, tools, employers, or dates not present in
  `resume-library/`.
- Change job titles or employment dates.
- Pad with buzzwords the base resume doesn't support.
